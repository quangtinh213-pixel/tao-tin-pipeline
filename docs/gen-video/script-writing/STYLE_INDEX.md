# STYLE_INDEX

Pick a narration voice without reading every profile. This file selects
**voice/tone** only. To choose the narrative arc first, read
`SCRIPT_PATTERNS.md`.

Styles are lightweight tone presets, not separate files. Apply the one that
fits by adjusting word choice and pacing in `plan.json`/`script.json`; do not
create new files per style.

## Pick a style by purpose

### Neutral briefing (default)

- Fits: `current-affairs-*`, `real-estate-civic-*`, official announcements,
  anything where accuracy must read as unquestionable.
- Tone: calm, precise, no editorializing.
- Borrow best: short declarative sentences, figures stated early, no
  rhetorical flourish.
- Avoid: humor, slang, or a strong personal opinion.

### Warm explainer

- Fits: `education-*`, `health-briefing-light`, `beauty-editorial-light`,
  anything explaining a topic to a general audience for the first time.
- Tone: friendly, patient, walks the viewer in gradually.
- Borrow best: soft transitions, plain-language definitions, gentle CTA.
- Avoid: being so soft that a genuinely urgent fact loses weight.

### Sharp analyst

- Fits: `business-*`, `tech-*`, market or industry pieces that need a strong
  point of view and a memorable closing line.
- Tone: direct, a little edgy, confident stance.
- Borrow best: tight hook, a strong closing takeaway, figures placed early.
- Avoid: over-claiming certainty on numbers that are still estimates or
  disputed.

### Energetic / event

- Fits: `entertainment-*`, `sports-*`, `auto-showroom-dark`, launches,
  matches, premieres, anything with genuine excitement to convey.
- Tone: upbeat, community energy, momentum.
- Borrow best: pace, enthusiasm on the result, clear call to keep watching.
- Avoid: applying hype-energy to a somber or risk-related story.

### Grounded caution

- Fits: `health-public-alert`, safety, financial risk, or any warning story.
- Tone: calm authority, transparent about severity without exaggerating.
- Borrow best: clear risk statement, concrete mitigation, no fear-mongering.
- Avoid: sounding alarmist or, at the other extreme, dismissive of real risk.

## Quick pick

- Needs sharp, memorable closing line: sharp analyst
- Needs soft, accessible teaching: warm explainer
- Needs unquestionable accuracy: neutral briefing
- Needs excitement and momentum: energetic / event
- Needs calm handling of risk: grounded caution

## Blending

Blend two only when the story genuinely needs both layers, and always keep
one primary and one secondary:

- sharp analyst + neutral briefing: strong takeaway, but every figure stays
  precise and sourced
- warm explainer + neutral briefing: accessible, but never softens a fact
  that matters
- energetic / event + neutral briefing: momentum without losing precision on
  scores, dates, or figures

Do not blend evenly. One style leads; the other only lends color.
